styles

A catalogue of 200 visual directions, each with type, colour, grid and motion, for choosing a style on purpose.

(01) What it is

The skill lists 200 styles across four files: classics, interfaces, print, minimalism, business and crafts, plus 3D and animation notes.

Each entry gives the origin, the idea, typography, colour, form and grid, motion, where it works and what to avoid.

Claude uses it when you ask for something "in the style of", want a style picked for a project, want two directions mixed, or want a new one invented.

(02) Three demos

Bauhaus

Geometry as language. Circle, square and triangle move with the scroll. Flat primary colours, no shading, a strict grid.

Ma

Japanese emptiness. Few elements, large gaps, a slow fade and a single hairline. The space between things is the design.
